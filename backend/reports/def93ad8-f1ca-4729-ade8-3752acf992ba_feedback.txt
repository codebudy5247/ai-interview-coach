  "A closure is the combination of a function bundled together with references to its surrounding state, known as the lexical environment. In JavaScript, closures allow an inner function to access variables from an outer function's scope even after that outer function has finished executing. A real-world example is data encapsulation: you can use a closure to create private variables that can only be modified through a specific returned function, such as a counter that hides its internal state from the global scope.
