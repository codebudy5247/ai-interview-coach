🏆 IDEAL ANSWER
  "A closure is the combination of a function bundled together with references to its surrounding state, known as the lexical environment. In JavaScript, a closure is created every time a function is defined, allowing it to 'remember' and access variables from its outer scope even after the outer function has finished executing. This is essential for data privacy, allowing developers to emulate private variables, and is widely used in functional programming patterns like currying and memoization."
